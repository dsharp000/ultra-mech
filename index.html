What's under the crown.

The core innovations and tactical upgrades that make the UM-34 chassis the most capable frame UltraMech has ever fielded.

Central power integration

SoulCore BlueSpark

Seated in the center of the chest behind reinforced crystal plating, the SoulCore turns dense core energy into instant motor torque and high-voltage weapon output.

  • Mounted at the center of mass for stability under heavy impacts.
  • The blue spark glows through armor seams: live core telemetry at a glance.
Premium factory option

Dual Upper Arm Linkage

Turns a two-arm chassis into a quad-weapon platform. Hydraulic servos give the upper mounts independent targeting while weight stays balanced over the hip drive.

  • 360° fire arc without fouling the lower arms.
  • Rated for high-recoil weapons like the Mini Gun and Giant Crossbow, at once.
Gold Crown exclusive

Gold Crown Command Crest

The signature three-spike crest atop the helmet is both a high-status command emblem and a long-range battlefield sensor array.

  • Twin amber beacons for squad recognition through smoke and fog.
  • Command-node optics relay targeting data to friendly units in real time.
Obsidian composite

Heavy-Duty Armor

Layered obsidian plating over a reinforced spine frame, built to take the hits a frontline titan draws.

  • Sloped shoulder guards deflect direct fire.
  • Reinforced spine carries all four arms under full recoil.
